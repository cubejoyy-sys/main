Privacy Policy — Scrap Pilot

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Scrap Pilot (com.dt.heliload) handles, and why. It applies to the Android version distributed through Google Play.

The short version

Scrap Pilot has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. It does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location. Your game progress stays on your device. Two things do leave the app: the advertisements it shows, and anonymous gameplay statistics we use to see which levels are too hard and where players stop. Neither of them carries your name, because the game never asks for it.

Information stored on your device

Your progress — levels completed, star ratings, in-game currency, purchased helicopters and upgrades, and settings — is saved locally on your device using Android's standard app storage. It is not uploaded anywhere, and it is not readable by us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game displays rewarded video adverts (which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit) and interstitial adverts between levels. Advertising is provided by Yodo1 MAS, which works with third-party advertising networks to select and deliver the adverts you see.

To deliver adverts and to measure and prevent fraud, these providers may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID);
  • technical device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, country, and the app version;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it (typically country or city level — not precise GPS location);
  • information about adverts shown to you, such as impressions, clicks and completions.

This information is collected and processed by Yodo1 and the advertising networks it works with, under their own privacy policies rather than this one. Yodo1's privacy notice for its MAS advertising service is available from Yodo1 on request, and we will provide a direct link here once published. You can also contact us at the address below and we will help direct any question about how advertising data is handled.

Analytics

So that we can tell which levels are too difficult, where players give up, and whether the game is stable, Scrap Pilot sends anonymous gameplay statistics to GameAnalytics, which processes them on our behalf.

What is sent is a record of what happened in the game, not of who you are:

  • levels started, completed and failed, how long each attempt took, how it ended (for example running out of fuel or crashing), and the star rating earned;
  • upgrades and helicopters bought with in-game currency, and adverts shown and whether a reward was given;
  • in-app purchases, as an amount and a currency, so we can see our own revenue — never any payment detail, which we are not given in the first place;
  • error and crash reports, so a fault that only happens on some devices can be found;
  • technical device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language and the country your IP address indicates;
  • a random identifier created when you install the game, which is what lets one player's twenty levels be counted as twenty levels rather than twenty players. It is not derived from anything about you, and reinstalling the game replaces it. Because the game also shows adverts, your device's advertising identifier and your limit-ad-tracking setting are sent alongside it.

We are not sent and do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. GameAnalytics acts only on our instructions and does not sell this data; see the GameAnalytics privacy notice for how they handle it.

Your advertising and analytics choices

You can limit how adverts are personalised at any time from your device: Settings → Privacy → Ads, where Android lets you reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of advert personalisation. Doing so does not remove adverts from the game, but it reduces how far they are tailored to you, and it applies to the advertising identifier sent with the analytics described above as well.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before personalised adverts are requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change that choice later from the privacy option shown in the consent form.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Google's handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Scrap Pilot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us using the address below and we will act on it.

Data retention and your rights

Scrap Pilot keeps no accounts and no server of its own, so there is no personal profile held by Dragan Trajkovski for you to export or correct. Your progress lives on your device and uninstalling the game deletes it immediately. What leaves the device is the advertising and analytics data described above, and you can have that deleted on request.

How to ask us to delete your data

Email cubejoyy@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request – Scrap Pilot”. To let us find the right records, please include:

  1. roughly when you played (a month is enough) and the country you played in;
  2. your device model;
  3. if you can, your advertising ID — on the device, under Settings → Google → Ads.

We ask for these because the analytics records are identified only by a random number created when the game was installed. We are not given your name, email address or account, so there is no way to look you up from them — which is also why we cannot delete records for someone who will not tell us roughly where and when to look.

We reply within 30 days.

What gets deleted, and what cannot be

  • Deleted on request: the gameplay analytics records described in the Analytics section — levels played, outcomes, adverts shown, purchases as an amount and currency, crash reports, and the random install identifier and device details attached to them. We instruct GameAnalytics to delete them; their data retention and deletion policy governs how long raw records are held in the meantime.
  • Deleted by you, instantly, without asking us: everything stored on your device — progress, stars, in-game currency, upgrades, purchased helicopters and settings. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes all of it. We never held a copy, so we cannot restore it afterwards.
  • Not ours to delete: data held by the advertising networks. Those are separate controllers with their own records. Reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads, which detaches your device from past advertising data, and see the Yodo1 and partner policies for their own deletion routes. Write to us anyway if you would like help directing such a request.
  • Kept regardless: records of purchases held by Google Play, which Google retains for tax and accounting reasons under the Google Privacy Policy. We have no control over those and cannot delete them.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with comparable law, these steps are also how you exercise your statutory rights of access, erasure and objection. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
